# Website brief: Teach Talent

## The business
- **Business name:** Teach Talent (Education Recruitment Agency)
- **What they do:** Recruit teaching assistants and SEN support staff for schools across the East of England.
- **Area served:** East of England: Cambridgeshire, Norfolk, Suffolk, Essex, Hertfordshire, Bedfordshire.
- **Client or prospect?** Pitch / prospect (concept build).

## The visitor
- **Ideal customer:** (1) Headteachers, SENCOs and business managers needing TAs / SEN support. (2) Teaching assistants, SEN TAs, HLTAs and LSAs looking for work.
- **The ONE thing:** Two paths: "Hire staff" (schools) and "Find a role" (candidates).
- **Where that goes:** Netlify Forms on /for-schools/#request and /for-candidates/#register, then /thanks/.
