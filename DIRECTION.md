# Direction: Quiet Prestige

- **Fonts:** Cormorant Garamond 300-500 (display, italic for emphasis), Manrope 400-700 (body/UI). Self-hosted.
- **Palette:** --cream #F6F3EC, --paper #FBF9F4, --navy #14223F, --gold #B08D57, --gold-ink #7F6133 (text-safe gold, 5.2:1 on cream), --ink #1B1F2A, --muted #5A6070, --line #DDD6C8.
- **Shape:** Square photos and panels, 999px pill buttons only. Hairline 1px rules.
- **Layout:** Flatline-style editorial: huge uppercase serif headline over a centred portrait photo, numbered (01)(02) role list with hairlines, floating "Get in touch" card bottom right on desktop. Wide margins.
- **Motion:** medium. Text rise and fade on reveal, image slow scale-in, ease-out cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1), UI <= 300ms, press scale(0.97). Reduced motion keeps fades only.
- **Dials:** variance 6, motion 5, density 3.
- **Dark mode:** ruled out (cream is the brand ground).

## Contract
- **Thesis:** The quiet confidence of a premium agency, applied to the people who hold classrooms together.
- **Own world:** cream paper, navy ink, gold mortarboard accents.
- **Story:** promise, two paths, roles, how it works, counties, voices, FAQ, CTA.
- **First viewport:** "THE TALENT BEHIND EVERY CLASSROOM" over a reading photo; two buttons.
- **Form:** two Netlify forms (school request, candidate register) plus contact and job alerts.
